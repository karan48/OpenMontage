# Script Guide: Cinematic (Hinglish documentaries — long-form and Shorts)

Read this after `skills/creative/storytelling.md`. Where they conflict on register, structure or humor level, this guide wins. Plain language still applies: no jargon without an image the viewer can picture.

*Draft. Lock it after the first two videos.*

## Voice and register

A storyteller leaning in close, not a narrator reading a script. Short sentences. Present tense for the dramatic part. Silence is a tool.

- "1987. एक छोटा सा गाँव. और एक रात… सब गायब."
- "Police ने file बंद कर दी. लेकिन एक चीज़ match नहीं कर रही थी."
- "अब ध्यान से सुनिए."

Use "आप" when addressing the viewer, sparingly: once to pull them in, once before the reveal.

## Long-form structure (10-20 min, draft)

Told like a film, not a lecture. Each chapter is a scene with a place, a time and a question.

| Part | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-60 s | **Cold open:** the strangest moment of the story, dropped in without context. End on a question. |
| ~60 s | **Title.** |
| Chapters 1-N (3-5) | Each chapter **escalates**. A new fact, witness or turn makes things stranger or raises the stakes. Every chapter ends on a question that pulls into the next one. |
| Final 15-20% | **The reveal**, or the darker unanswered question. Slow down and give it room. |
| Last 30-60 s | **Epilogue** that loops back to the cold open's image or line, so the viewer sees it differently now. |

Use chapter cards (place and year) as on-screen anchors. Silence, room tone and music carry long stretches; don't narrate over everything.

## Shorts structure (45-60 s)

A Short is either a standalone mini-story or a cut-down of a long film's cold open or reveal. A cut-down's first line is re-voiced so it works cold, and it ends on a loop-back line rather than "watch the full video" alone.

| Time | Beat | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 s | **Hook** | The strangest image plus one line. No title, no "आज हम बात करेंगे". |
| 2-10 s | **Context** | Who, where, when, in one breath. |
| 10-40 s | **Escalation** | 2-3 steps, each stranger or higher-stakes than the last. Something new every 8-12 s. |
| 40-55 s | **Reveal** | The answer, or the darker question. Slow down, then a pause before the key line. |
| last 2-3 s | **Loop-back** | A line that sends the viewer back to the first frame ("…और वो गाँव? वो आज भी वहीं है."). |

## Hooks

Work well here:
- An impossible-sounding fact.
- An object that shouldn't exist.
- A date plus a disappearance.
- "ये photo असली है."

Avoid:
- Questions the viewer can answer "no" to.
- Clickbait the reveal doesn't pay off.

## Humor

Low. At most one dry, ironic line. Never at a victim's expense.

## Never

- Graphic description of violence or death.
- Invented facts presented as true.
- An AI face of a real named person.
- An ending without a loop-back.
