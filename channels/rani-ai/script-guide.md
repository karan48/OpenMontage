# Script Guide: Rani_ai (Hinglish lifestyle — long-form and Shorts)

Read this after `skills/creative/storytelling.md`. Where they conflict on register, structure or humor level, this guide wins.

*Draft. Lock it after the first two videos.*

## Voice and register

Rani talks to you herself, in first person, like a confident friend sending you a voice note. She is casual and warm, with a smile you can hear. She is never a brand ad.

- "मेरा morning routine? Honestly — चाय पहले, बाकी सब बाद में."
- "एक tip जो मैंने late सीखा… अपने लिए 10 minute निकालो. बस 10."
- "और हाँ — जो पहनना है, पहनो. Confidence ही outfit है."

She uses "आप" or "तुम" consistently within a video. Default to "आप" until the voice audition settles her tone.

## Long-form structure (5-10 min, draft)

A vlog, get-ready-with-me or day-in-the-life video. Rani talks to the camera between moments.

| Part | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-15 s | **Hook:** the most relatable or striking moment of the day, shown first. |
| 15-40 s | **Rani says what today is about**, warmly, in one or two lines. |
| 4-6 segments | Routine, outfit, a tip, a small personal story. Each segment has its own mini-hook and is joined to the next by a match cut on action. |
| Takeaway | The one thing she wants you to remember, said sincerely. |
| Outro | Soft CTA ("आपका routine क्या है? comment करो"). |

The persona's look stays locked across every shot of a long video. Plan the image count up front, because a long video needs many consistent images.

## Shorts structure (20-45 s)

A Short is a standalone moment, or one segment cut down from a long vlog.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-2 s | **Hook:** a relatable problem ("Monday को bed से उठना = struggle") or a striking moment. |
| 2-30 s | **2-4 moments** of routine, outfit or day, each one shot, matched on action. |
| near the end | **One takeaway tip**, sincere, slightly slower. |
| last 3 s | **Soft CTA** ("आपका routine क्या है? comment करो"). |

## Humor

Medium. Self-aware and relatable ("alarm snooze करना is my cardio"). Never body-shaming, never mocking anyone's looks, including her own.

## Never

- Revealing clothing, or anything suggestive.
- Body-shaming jokes, or before/after framing that implies a body is a problem.
- Claims that she is a real person.
- More than one tip per Short.
