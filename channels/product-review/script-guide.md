# Script Guide: Product Review (Hinglish — long-form reviews and Shorts)

Read this after `skills/creative/storytelling.md`. Where they conflict on register, structure or humor level, this guide wins. Plain language still applies: translate every spec into what it means for the buyer ("5000 mAh — मतलब पूरा दिन, आराम से").

*Draft. Lock it after the first two videos.*

## Voice and register

The friend who bought it so you don't waste money. Quick, confident and honest. Say the price out loud. Say who it's NOT for.

- "₹15,999 में ये phone… worth it? Short answer — हाँ, लेकिन एक catch है."
- "Camera daylight में solid है. Night में? थोड़ा struggle करता है."
- "अगर आप gamer हैं — skip करो. बाकी सबके लिए — buy."

## Long-form structure (8-12 min, draft)

An in-depth review in chapters. Viewers skip to what they care about, so each chapter must stand on its own and gets a YouTube chapter timestamp.

| Part | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-15 s | **Verdict-tease hook**, plus the one thing that surprised you most. |
| 15-45 s | **Price, plus who it's for**, plus what you tested and for how long. |
| Chapters | **One per category that matters** (design and build, display, performance, camera, battery, software…). Each one has a claim, proof on screen, and what it means for the buyer, then ends with a one-line mini-verdict. |
| vs alternatives | 1-2 rivals at the same price, compared in a side-by-side table. |
| Recap | Pros and cons, with icons. |
| Who should buy / skip | Be specific about the buyer type. |
| Verdict | Full-screen verdict card with a score. |

Disclose sponsorship, free review units and affiliate links in the first 30 s when they apply.

## Shorts structure (40-60 s)

A Short is a standalone quick verdict, or a cut-down of the long review's verdict and its strongest pro/con.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-2 s | **Verdict-tease hook:** "Worth it? Short answer…" or the product's single most surprising fact. |
| 2-10 s | **Price, plus who it's for.** |
| 10-35 s | **2-3 pros**, each with proof on screen (spec, test or footage). |
| 35-45 s | **1-2 cons**, said plainly. |
| 45-55 s | **Who should buy, who should skip.** |
| 55-60 s | **Verdict card** plus a score. |

## Humor

Low. At most one wry line about a real flaw ("charger box में नहीं है — classic"). Never mock buyers or rival fans.

## Never

- Claims without proof on screen.
- Hiding a sponsorship or affiliate link.
- An AI-generated image of the product.
- A review with no cons.
- Specs without saying what they mean for the buyer.
