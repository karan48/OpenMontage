# Script Guide: Young Story (Hinglish young-adult drama and romance — long-form and Shorts)

Read this after `skills/creative/storytelling.md`. Where they conflict on register, structure or humor level, this guide wins.

*Draft. Lock it after the first two stories.*

## Voice and register

The **narrator** is close, warm and a little wistful, like a friend telling you what happened to someone they know. They set the scene and carry time jumps and feelings the pictures can't show. They never explain what a character's line already said.

- "उस दिन बारिश हो रही थी… और Riya को पता नहीं था कि ये आख़िरी बार होगा."
- "कुछ दिन पहले. College का पहला दिन."

**Characters** talk like real 18-25-year-olds in Hinglish: short lines, interruptions, texting slang where it fits.

- Aarav: "Sorry यार, late हो गया— तुम ठीक हो?"
- Riya: "[whispers] मैं ठीक नहीं हूँ, Aarav."

Tag every dialogue line with its speaker and an emotion cue (`[whispers]`, `[crying]`, `[laughs]`, `[angry]`) so each voice is rendered right. Keep lines to one or two sentences; let silence and music carry the rest.

## Long-form structure (8-15 min, draft)

| Part | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-20 s | **Flash-forward cold open:** the emotional peak (a confession, a slammed door, someone crying in the rain), with no context. |
| ~20 s | **"कुछ दिन पहले…"**, then the title card. |
| Setup | Who they are, and what they want. Make the viewer care within 60 s. |
| Complications (2-3) | Each makes it harder: a misunderstanding, family pressure, a rival, distance. |
| Turning point | The confession, betrayal or choice the cold open showed, now landing with full weight. |
| Aftermath | What it costs them. Slow down here. |
| Ending | A resolution that earns its feeling, or a cliffhanger into the next part ("Part 2 में…"). |

Multi-part stories open each part with a 10-15 s "पिछले part में" recap.

## Shorts structure (45-60 s)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-2 s | **Emotional hook line:** "उसने कहा था वो कभी नहीं जाएगी." |
| 2-20 s | **Setup in one breath:** who, and what's at stake. |
| 20-45 s | **The twist or confession.** |
| 45-60 s | **Gut-punch ending**, or a "Part 2?" cliffhanger. |

A cut-down takes an episode's emotional peak, opens on its strongest line (re-voiced to work cold), and ends on the cliffhanger with a pointer to the full episode.

## Humor

Low. Light banter between friends adds warmth in setup scenes. Never joke during the turning point or the aftermath.

## Never

- Sexual content, or romance involving anyone under 18.
- Stalking, possessiveness or violence presented as love.
- Self-harm shown as a method or glamorised. Any story touching it ends with the Tele-MANAS 14416 card.
- Real, identifiable people.
- The narrator reading out a line the character already said.
